# Voice

Bimble talks like a good site engineer: plain, short, specific, friendly.

- **Buttons are verbs with an object**: "Place slab", "Export IFC", "Save". Not "OK", "Submit", "Proceed".
- **Chips are nouns with numbers**: "Not saved: 2 edits", "Linked: Architecture".
- **Use the trade's words**: slab, storey, axis, formwork, C30/37, 1:50, IfcSlab. Don't explain them.
- **Errors say what happened, then what to do**: "Error: the file is not IFC 4. Open it in … or convert it first." The word "Error" comes first because the colour (ochre) is shared with warnings.
- **No blame, no exclamation marks, no "Oops".**
- **Sentence case.** Numbers as digits, a space before units ("250 mm").
- The app ships in English and French. Keep labels short enough to survive French (+20–30% length).
