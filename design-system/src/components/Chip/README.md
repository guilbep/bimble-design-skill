# Chip

Status chips and dots: a disc colour plus words, so the state never rests on colour alone.

- **Chip**: pill, 3px 12px, `ink` text 14px on `attention`, `reference`, `success` or `selection`. Always a noun, often with a number: "Not saved: 2 edits", "Linked: Architecture".
- **Status dot**: a `dot`-sized disc with a 1px `ink` outline (pink and ochre are too pale on cream without it), followed by its label. Used at the foot of panels.

Chips sit at the right of the top bar, before the primary button.
